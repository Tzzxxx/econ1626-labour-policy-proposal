Generative AI was used primarily for discussions and learning. I shared views on generative AI and Australian jobs and asked whether the perspective chosen would be suitable for a policy proposal for Jobs and Skills Australia. AI assisted me in understanding the assessment requirements, explained terms like task exposure, displacement and productivity and compared the policy alternatives. It offered me some wording for certain parts of the proposal. 
I individually checked the sources and data provided in the proposal and made sure that all references supported claims that were included in my proposal. That was important since sometimes AI can misunderstand sources, provide claims that cannot be found there and state possible effects as certain ones. My final recommendation for a two-year monitoring pilot was my own choice based on analysis of the policy alternatives, their feasibility, cost and the need for better evidence on Australian workplaces.

AI helped me formulate my thinking and pinpoint issues that should be investigated further. It did not remove my obligation for writing the analysis and supporting evidence. I learned to use AI critically and asked specific questions, checked suggestions and made my decisions personally.
